Apple Podcasts

Many episodes show a time-synced transcript in the Podcasts app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can read and search it in-app; Apple documents sharing or copying up to 200 words with attribution, not a full-episode download. Step-by-step: How to get an Apple Podcasts transcript. For a full file from a public Apple link, use Apple Podcasts transcript.

Spotify

Some shows offer Spotify’s Read Along / episode transcript in Now Playing on mobile and sometimes on desktop. Listeners cannot export that text as .txt or .srt. Details: How to get a Spotify podcast transcript. When the show also has a public RSS feed, paste the Spotify link into Podskrift. True exclusives and subscriber-only episodes without a public feed cannot be transcribed — see Spotify episode without RSS.

RSS feeds and other apps

Every public podcast has an RSS feed. Publishers can attach a transcript with the Podcasting 2.0 podcast:transcript tag (podcasting2.org docs). Check the feed or the show’s website first. If there is audio but no transcript file, paste the feed URL into RSS to text or use podcast to text with any public link. Need the feed URL? How to find an RSS feed.

Video podcasts

Podskrift transcribes the public audio feed for a show — not a pasted YouTube watch URL or a video-only page with no audio enclosure. If the video podcast also publishes audio in RSS (most do), that path works. See video podcast transcript.

Which method should I use?

Situation Try first Then
Listening in Apple Podcasts In-app Transcript button Publisher RSS file, or paste the Apple link
Listening on Spotify Read Along in Now Playing Public feed via Spotify link, or check if there is no RSS
Any public show / other apps Show site or podcast:transcript in RSS Podcast to text or RSS to text
Video podcast with an audio feed Same as RSS / Apple / Spotify link Video podcast transcript
Exclusive, subscriber-only or private feed Publisher’s own download, if any Not available via public-feed tools
