What it costs

Uses your Podskrift minutes (trial, credit pack, or your own OpenAI key). Episodes you've already transcribed are free to fetch again on your account. Full pricing.

Troubleshooting

  • Sign-in loop — Finish Allow in the Podskrift window; if it keeps bouncing, open Settings → Connected apps on the website, then click Connect again in Claude.
  • Not available on your plan — Free accounts get one custom connector. On Team/Enterprise an Owner must add Podskrift under Organization settings → Connectors before members can connect.
  • Revoke access — On Podskrift, go to Settings → Connected apps and revoke Claude. You can reconnect later.
