On iPhone and Android

  1. Play the episode

    Open the episode in the Spotify app and start playback. The transcript only appears once the episode is playing or loaded in Now Playing.

  2. Open full-screen Now Playing

    Tap the mini player at the bottom so the episode fills the screen.

  3. Scroll to the transcript

    Scroll down past the controls. On some versions a transcript / Read Along card replaces the cover art; on others it sits under About this episode. Tap the card to read along while the audio plays.

On desktop and the web player

  1. Play the episode in the Spotify desktop app or at open.spotify.com.
  2. Open the Now Playing panel on the right (the control near the bottom-right of the player).
  3. Scroll that panel. Where a transcript exists, Read Along / Episode transcript appears there.

Some auto-generated transcripts are available on mobile only. If you see one on your phone and nothing on desktop, that can be expected.

Why the transcript is missing

  • Eligibility — Spotify generates transcripts only for select shows, not the whole catalogue.
  • Language — auto transcripts cover a limited set of languages (Spotify for Creators lists eligibility for select English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese shows).
  • Creator turned it off — in Spotify for Creators, publishers can disable transcripts per episode or for the show.
  • Desktop limits — not every transcript that appears on mobile is shown on desktop or the web player.
  • App version — older clients may hide the control; update Spotify and try again before concluding there is none.

You cannot copy or export Spotify’s listener transcript

Spotify’s Read Along text is for following along in the app. Listeners cannot select the full transcript, download .txt or .srt, or export it into another tool. Spotify may offer sharing a short quote from Read Along; that is not a full-episode export.

Creators with access to Spotify for Creators can view, download or upload transcripts for their own eligible episodes — that path is for show owners, not listeners.

Alternatives

  • Podskrift — Paste the Spotify episode or show link. When the show also publishes a public RSS feed (most do), you get a transcript you can copy and download as .txt and .srt — including episodes where Spotify shows no Read Along. Spotify podcast transcript.
  • Apple Podcasts or the public RSS feed — If the show is listed outside Spotify, you can paste that link instead. See Apple Podcasts or how to find an RSS feed.
  • Spotify exclusives / subscriber-only — No public feed means no audio Podskrift can fetch. Those episodes cannot be transcribed here.
