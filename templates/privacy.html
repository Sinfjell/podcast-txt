Privacy

Podskrift is made by Nettsmed (Fjellestad AS), Kristiansand, Norway, which is responsible for the personal data described here.

What we store

Your account email, a password hash, your optional OpenAI API key, and the transcripts you create. Your OpenAI key is used only to call OpenAI on your behalf; we never send it to analytics.

Services that process data for us

  • OpenAI: episode audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API to produce the transcript.
  • PostHog (EU Cloud): product analytics and session replay, so we can see where signup-to-first-transcript drops off — only after you Accept cookies. Replays mask form inputs (including email, password and API key fields) and transcript text. We identify you only by an internal user id, not by email or name, and we do not send API keys, passwords, emails or transcript text as event properties. If you Decline, we do not load analytics cookies or record sessions.
  • Stripe: card payments for optional credit packs. Stripe receives the payment details you enter on their Checkout page; we store only a purchase record (Stripe session id, amount and minutes credited), never your card number.
  • Mailgun (EU region): sends transcript-ready emails. Every such email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Sentry: error reports, with cookies, headers, IP addresses and form bodies stripped before they are sent.
  • Hetzner: hosting, in the EU.

Fonts are served from podskrift.com itself, not from Google.

Cookies and local storage

  • session and remember_token: keep you logged in. Necessary for the site to work.
  • podskrift_cookie_consent (cookie and local storage, kept for 12 months): remembers whether you Accepted or Declined analytics. Necessary to honour your choice. Change it any time via Cookie settings in the footer.
  • PostHog's ph_… cookie and local storage: an anonymous analytics id and session replay state. Set only after you Accept. Before that (and if you Decline), PostHog runs in memory-only mode with capturing off — no analytics cookies and no session recording.
  • Local storage for small preferences, such as your last spoken language and which What's new items you have seen.

Shared transcripts

If you share a transcript, anyone with the link can read it until you turn sharing off. Share links are kept out of search engines.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, or have your account and transcripts deleted. Contact us below. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
