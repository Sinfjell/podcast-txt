How to Find RSS URLs for Podcasts

Quick Start

Found a podcast on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, or another app? Here's how to find the RSS URL you need.

Apple Podcasts
  1. Open Apple Podcasts or visit podcasts.apple.com
  2. Find the podcast and click "Share" > "Copy Link"
  3. Paste it on the home page — we convert it automatically!
Spotify

Spotify URLs are not RSS feeds.

You need the original RSS feed from the podcast's website.

  1. Visit the podcast's official website
  2. Look for "RSS", "Feed", or "Subscribe" links
  3. Copy the RSS feed URL
Other Platforms
  1. Visit the podcast's official website
  2. Look for "RSS", "Feed", or "Subscribe" links
  3. Right-click and "Copy link address"
  4. URLs usually end with .rss or .xml
Pro Tips

Works: Direct RSS feeds, Apple Podcasts URLs (converted), Simplecast, Anchor feeds

Doesn't work: Spotify URLs, YouTube URLs, social media links
