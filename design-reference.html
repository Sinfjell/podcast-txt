Why a transcript beats a recording

A podcast episode is a long stretch of time. Forty-eight minutes of audio can hold three good ideas and one sentence you will want to quote, and the only way to find any of them again is to listen from the start. A transcript turns that stretch of time into a page. You can search it for a name, skim it for the part about pricing, and copy the exact words into your notes without pausing and rewinding.

Podskrift downloads the audio for the episode you pick and runs it through OpenAI Whisper. The work happens on our side, so you can close the tab and come back later; the job keeps going and the result is waiting in your history. Long episodes are split into parts and stitched back together, which is why the progress bar counts parts rather than minutes.

You pay for minutes of audio, not for a month of access. New accounts get 60 minutes free on our key. After that you can buy a pack of 300 minutes for $5, or add your own OpenAI API key and pay OpenAI directly at about $0.36 per hour of audio. There is no subscription to cancel and nothing to remember to turn off.

The text comes back with timestamps, so every line points to a moment in the recording. Download it as plain text for reading, or as an SRT file when you need subtitles. If the language is detected wrongly, name it before you start and run the episode again; unused trial minutes are returned when you cancel a running job.
